Publication

Nature · npj Urban Sustainability Open Access Published 17 July 2026

TAL-Net: a temporal attention LSTM framework for urban electricity and emissions forecasting in the U.S.

Ann Mary Thomas, Maitreyee Dey & Soumya Prakash Rana · GENESIS Research Lab, London Metropolitan University

Urban sustainability depends critically on accurate forecasting of electricity demand and associated CO&sub2; emissions, particularly as cities strive to meet climate targets while ensuring energy resilience. This study presents TAL-Net, a novel Temporal Attention LSTM Network designed for joint forecasting of electricity demand and CO&sub2; emissions in complex urban energy systems — evaluated across California and Texas using high-resolution EIA grid data. TAL-Net consistently achieves superior forecasting accuracy, with attention mechanisms capturing temporal patterns and cross-variable dependencies that conventional models miss.
