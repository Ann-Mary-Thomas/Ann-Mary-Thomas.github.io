👋 About Me

Hello! I'm Ann Mary Thomas, a data analyst and researcher passionate about turning data into actionable strategies for sustainability and innovation. With a foundation in Mechanical Automobile Engineering and a Master's in Data Analytics, I bring a rare combination of technical expertise, research acumen, and real-world problem-solving to every project.

I'm currently working as an Energy Analyst at Ameresco, Inc. - a leading global energy solutions provider - within their Measurement & Verification (M&V) team in London. In this role I develop data-driven energy performance models, build automated Python pipelines, and produce dashboards and reports that evidence energy and carbon savings across Ameresco's public sector, healthcare, and utilities portfolio.

This role builds on a strong industry foundation. Most recently, I worked as an Energy Consumption and Sustainability Analyst at WMG, University of Warwick, deployed to 3T Additive Manufacturing as part of the £14.1M government-funded STRATA programme. There I independently built a full energy performance baseline, designed six decarbonisation scenarios with 5-year ROI projections, and delivered Power BI dashboards for Honeywell consortium partners - all aligned to ISO 50001 and validated against ND-NEED 2022 and CIBSE Guide F benchmarks.

That work built directly on my internship at 3T Additive Manufacturing (Sep–Dec 2025), where I achieved a 39.3% carbon footprint reduction through systematic data analysis and targeted energy-saving strategies - demonstrating how data-driven approaches can deliver measurable sustainability results in real manufacturing environments.

Alongside my industry experience, I conduct research at London Metropolitan University's GENESIS Lab, where I designed TAL-Net - a Temporal Attention LSTM Network - achieving 0.48% MAPE for electricity demand forecasting and a 19× improvement over ARIMA. This work has resulted in two co-authored journal papers currently under peer review.

I thrive on building clear, impactful narratives from complex datasets - whether through interactive dashboards, predictive models, or scenario-based analysis. My curiosity drives me to explore emerging techniques in time-series forecasting, deep learning, and renewable energy analytics, always with the aim of making insights accessible and actionable to stakeholders.

Beyond the numbers, I enjoy mentoring peers, designing intuitive visualisations, and advocating for data literacy among non-technical audiences. I believe that the most powerful data stories don't just inform - they inspire change and connect communities to a smarter, more sustainable future.
